Phase 1 — Contact & Relationship Database
Contact Entity
Configure the Contact entity to support CRE relationship management, including:
• Multiple simultaneous relationship classifications a contact may belong to more than one category:
◦ Broker, Landlord, Tenant, Investor, Developer, Property Manager, Asset Manager
◦ Lender, Attorney, Architect, Engineer, Vendor, Consultant, Municipality
◦ Economic Development, Franchisee, Franchise Development, Owner/User
◦ General Contractor, Government Agency
• Professional designation tags searchable and reportable: SIOR, CCIM, ICSC, CRE, CPM, RPA, LEED, NAIOP, ULI, BOMA, MBA, ALC
• Custom fields: Business Line, Assigned Broker, Markets Served, Geographic Coverage, Target Markets, Property Preferences, Min/Max SF, Lease Expiration Date, Renewal Timeline, Referral Source, Relationship Tier, Last Meaningful Contact, Preferred Communication Method, Social Media Links, Tags



Account Company Entity
Configure accounts to support multiple classifications and CRE-specific data:
• Classifications: Tenant, Landlord, Developer, REIT, Investment Group, Family Office, Franchise, Brokerage, Municipality, Property Owner, Vendor, Lender, Contractor
• Fields: Portfolio SF, Markets, Industries, NAICS Code, Multiple Office Locations
• Relationships: Linked contacts, linked properties, ownership relationships



Custom Entity: Property Record
Create a custom Property entity with the following field groups:
• Property Information: Address, County, Market, Submarket, Property Type, Ownership, Broker Assignment, Status
• Building Information: Building SF, Land Area, Occupancy %, Parking Ratio, Construction Year, Zoning
• Leasing Information: Available Suites, Lease Rates, Operating Expenses, Tenant Improvements, Leasing Status
• Ownership: Multiple owners, ownership entities, property managers, asset managers
• Multi-Tenant Support: Suite numbers, office stacking, retail tenant rosters, lease dates, expiration dates, options, vacancies
• Relationship views: Property → Tenants, Tenant → Properties, Landlord → Portfolio



Views & Search Configuration
Build saved views including:
• Tenant requirements by SF range searchable/filterable
• Lease expirations — next 6, 12, and 18 months
• Stale contacts Last Activity greater 60 days, Tier A or B
• Available listings by market, submarket, and SF
• Landlord portfolio by owner/account
• Contacts by professional designation e.g., all SIOR members
