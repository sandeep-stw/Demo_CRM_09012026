Phase 3 — Outlook-First Workflow (Highest Priority)
This is our most critical requirement. Brokers must be able to complete everyday CRM tasks without leaving Outlook.



Capabilities Required
• Deploy Dynamics 365 App for Outlook to all users (desktop, web, and mobile)
• Track emails to CRM contact and deal records with one click
• Create contacts directly from Outlook email signatures
• Create opportunities directly from emails
• Associate emails with deals, properties, and companies
• Schedule meetings and link to CRM records
• Create follow-up tasks without leaving Outlook
• View full CRM activity history, related contacts, and open opportunities from the Outlook sidebar
• Automatic suggestion of matching CRM records for incoming email senders



Power Automate Flows — Outlook
• Auto-log accepted calendar events to linked contact and opportunity records
• Alert broker when email arrives from a sender not in the CRM, with one-click contact creation
